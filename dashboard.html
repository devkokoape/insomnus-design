I · THE PASS

Three tiers. Three jobs.

Gen 1 never burns. Everything above it does.

Gen 1 is the access pass — fixed, required to breed, stays in your wallet. Gen 2 is born from it and can be sold. Gen 3+ is the sink: burn three Gen 2s to climb.

Gen 1 fixed Gen 2 grows Gen 3 scarce

II · THE COST

Essence plus a Key.

One input rewards play. One input makes a market.

Essence is play-only. You cannot buy it. Keys drop rarely, or you buy them from players who farmed them. Gen 1 → 2 keeps both pets. Gen 2 → 3 burns three.

2 Gen 1 + Key + 10k 30-day pair lock 3 Gen 2 burn + 30k

III · NO PET

The dungeon still pays you.

Active players eat. Not only owners.

Sell keys to holders. Rent a pet, keep the drops. Weekly and monthly pools take most key spend — any wallet can fight for them.

Sell keys Rent a pet Open pools

IV · HOLD

Own it. Then pick a path.

Breed. Upgrade. Or rent and sleep.

Pair Gen 1s for a tradeable Gen 2. Burn three Gen 2s for a scarcer Gen 3 with better finds and gated nights. Or skip the grind and lease the pet.

It does not inflate to dust.

Gen 3 eats Gen 2. New gens wake the market.

Supply grows, then burns. Fresh generations every two to three months start breed demand and key flow again. No single mint has to carry the floor forever.
