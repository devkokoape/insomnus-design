FAQ

01

What are Insomnus Pets?

Insomnus Pets are collectible companions that boost your drop rate and unlock breeding. Holding one gives you an edge in loot chests; it's not just cosmetic.

02

How do I get a pet?

Mint runs in four rounds: Free Claim (Ancient One / OG holders and early Twitter supporters), GTD, Petlisted (earned via Discord level 3 + 1 invite, or Twitter raffles), then Public. Each round runs 30 minutes, and Public stays open until sold out.

03

What do pets do in-game?

Every pet boosts your drop rate from loot chests, giving holders better odds at common, uncommon, epic, and legendary items. It's a real gameplay advantage, not just a collectible.

04

What does breeding actually do?

Breeding combines two different Gen 1 pets (plus a Key and Essence) to produce a Gen 2 pet. Your Gen 1 pets aren't consumed — they just go on a one-month cooldown after each breed.

05

What's the difference between Gen 1, Gen 2, and Gen 3?

Gen 1 is the original mint and never runs out. Gen 2 comes only from breeding and is fully tradeable. Gen 3 comes from burning three different Gen 2 pets and carries stronger resource-find bonuses plus access to gated events — it's the scarce tier.

06

Where does Key spending go?

It's split three ways: development, marketing, and the majority into community rewards — weekly and monthly tournaments and boss events that any player can compete in.
